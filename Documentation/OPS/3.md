# Meet v1.0

# Operations & Monitoring Manual (OPS)

## OPS - Part 3

## Incident Management, Recovery & Operational Procedures

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Operations & Monitoring Manual

**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the incident management process, recovery procedures, operational runbooks, disaster recovery operations, maintenance activities, escalation procedures, and service restoration framework for the Meet platform.

The objective is to minimize service disruption, restore normal operations quickly, and continuously improve operational resilience.

---

# 2. Incident Management Objectives

The incident management framework shall provide:

* Rapid Incident Detection
* Structured Response
* Service Restoration
* Root Cause Analysis
* Operational Documentation
* Continuous Improvement

Incident handling shall follow documented operational procedures.

---

# 3. Incident Classification

Incidents shall be classified as:

* Critical
* High
* Medium
* Low
* Informational

Classification shall determine response priority and escalation requirements.

---

# 4. Incident Response Workflow

```text id="incidentresponseworkflow"
Incident Detected
        ↓
Classification
        ↓
Acknowledgement
        ↓
Investigation
        ↓
Containment
        ↓
Recovery
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Closure
```

Every incident shall receive a unique tracking identifier.

---

# 5. Escalation Matrix

Escalation shall follow:

* Operations Engineer
* Senior Operations Engineer
* System Administrator
* Technical Lead
* Engineering Manager
* Executive Notification (Critical Incidents)

Escalation paths shall be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# 6. Service Recovery

Recovery procedures shall support:

* Application Restart
* Queue Recovery
* Scheduler Recovery
* Database Recovery
* Storage Recovery
* Network Recovery

Recovery actions shall prioritize restoration of critical business services.

---

# 7. Disaster Recovery Operations

Operational disaster recovery shall include:

* Infrastructure Recovery
* Database Restoration
* File Restoration
* Configuration Recovery
* Application Redeployment
* Validation Testing

Recovery procedures shall be tested periodically.

---

# 8. Operational Runbooks

Runbooks shall exist for:

* Application Startup
* Application Shutdown
* Queue Worker Recovery
* Scheduler Recovery
* Backup Restoration
* Certificate Renewal
* Deployment Recovery

Runbooks shall remain synchronized with production infrastructure.

---

# 9. Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Every major incident shall include:

* Timeline of Events
* Root Cause
* Impact Assessment
* Corrective Actions
* Preventive Actions
* Lessons Learned

RCA reports shall be retained for future operational improvements.

---

# 10. Maintenance Procedures

Routine maintenance shall include:

* Operating System Updates
* Dependency Updates
* Database Optimization
* Log Rotation
* Storage Cleanup
* Backup Verification
* Security Patch Installation

Maintenance windows shall be scheduled and communicated.

---

# 11. Operational Checklists

Daily checklist:

* Verify Application Health
* Verify Database Connectivity
* Verify Queue Processing
* Verify Scheduler Execution
* Review Critical Alerts
* Verify Backup Status

Weekly checklist:

* Review Capacity Trends
* Review Error Logs
* Review Security Logs
* Validate Recovery Procedures

Monthly checklist:

* Disaster Recovery Validation
* Performance Review
* Infrastructure Audit
* Documentation Review

---

# 12. Incident Documentation

Each incident record shall include:

* Incident ID
* Detection Time
* Severity
* Affected Services
* Recovery Actions
* Resolution Time
* Final Status

Documentation shall support audits and future investigations.

---

# 13. Operational Communication

Communication procedures shall support:

* Internal Incident Notifications
* Stakeholder Updates
* Maintenance Announcements
* Service Restoration Notices

Communication shall be timely and accurate.

---

# 14. Success Metrics

Operational performance may be measured using:

* Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
* Mean Time to Acknowledge (MTTA)
* Mean Time to Recover (MTTR)
* Incident Frequency
* Service Availability
* Repeat Incident Rate

Metrics shall guide operational improvements.

---

# 15. Future Enhancements

The operational framework supports:

* Automated Incident Response
* Self-Healing Infrastructure
* AI-Assisted Root Cause Analysis
* Predictive Maintenance
* Automated Runbook Execution

Future capabilities shall integrate without redesigning operational processes.

---

# 16. OPS-3 Summary

This document defines the incident management lifecycle, operational runbooks, disaster recovery operations, maintenance procedures, root cause analysis framework, operational communication, and continuous improvement strategy for the Meet platform.
