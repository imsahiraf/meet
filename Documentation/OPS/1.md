# Meet v1.0

# Operations & Monitoring Manual (OPS)

## OPS - Part 1

## Operations Strategy & Monitoring Architecture

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Operations & Monitoring Manual

**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the operational strategy, monitoring architecture, operational governance, service health management, alerting framework, and day-to-day operational procedures for the Meet platform.

Its objective is to ensure reliable production operations, proactive monitoring, rapid incident detection, and continuous service availability.

---

# 2. Operational Objectives

The operations framework shall provide:

* Continuous Service Availability
* Proactive Monitoring
* Operational Visibility
* Incident Detection
* Capacity Awareness
* Service Reliability
* Continuous Improvement

Operations shall support stable production environments.

---

# 3. Operational Architecture

```text id="operationsarchitecture"
Users
      ↓
Application
      ↓
Monitoring Services
      ↓
Alerting
      ↓
Operations Team
      ↓
Incident Response
      ↓
Recovery
```

Operational events shall be monitored continuously.

---

# 4. Operational Responsibilities

Operations shall manage:

* Application Availability
* Infrastructure Health
* Database Operations
* Queue Processing
* Scheduler
* Storage
* Backups
* Monitoring
* Incident Management

Operational responsibilities shall be clearly documented.

---

# 5. Monitoring Scope

Production monitoring shall include:

* Application Health
* API Availability
* Database Health
* Queue Workers
* Scheduler Execution
* Storage Capacity
* Voice Services
* Video Services
* Authentication Services

Monitoring shall detect abnormal conditions promptly.

---

# 6. Health Checks

Health validation shall verify:

* Application Startup
* Database Connectivity
* Cache Availability
* Queue Availability
* Scheduler Execution
* Storage Accessibility

Health checks shall execute automatically at configured intervals.

---

# 7. Alert Management

Alerts shall be generated for:

* Service Failure
* Queue Failure
* Database Failure
* Storage Capacity
* High Error Rate
* Security Events
* Backup Failure

Alert severity shall support operational prioritization.

---

# 8. Operational Dashboards

Dashboards shall display:

* System Health
* Active Users
* Queue Status
* Scheduler Status
* Error Rates
* Storage Usage
* API Performance
* Call Activity

Operational dashboards shall support real-time decision making.

---

# 9. Logging Strategy

Operational logs shall include:

* Application Logs
* Queue Logs
* Scheduler Logs
* Security Logs
* Deployment Logs
* Audit Logs

Logs shall support investigation and troubleshooting.

---

# 10. Capacity Planning

Capacity reviews shall evaluate:

* User Growth
* Storage Growth
* Database Growth
* Queue Throughput
* API Utilization
* Media Usage

Capacity planning shall guide future infrastructure expansion.

---

# 11. Operational Reviews

Regular reviews shall include:

* Incident Analysis
* Performance Review
* Capacity Review
* Security Review
* Backup Review
* Documentation Review

Review outcomes shall be documented.

---

# 12. Documentation Requirements

Operations documentation shall include:

* Runbooks
* Incident Procedures
* Recovery Procedures
* Monitoring Configuration
* Alert Configuration

Operational documentation shall remain current.

---

# 13. Future Enhancements

The operational architecture supports:

* AI-Assisted Monitoring
* Predictive Alerting
* Automated Recovery
* Distributed Monitoring
* Cloud Operations
* Multi-Region Monitoring

Future capabilities shall integrate without redesigning operational processes.

---

# 14. OPS-1 Summary

This document establishes the operational strategy, monitoring architecture, health management, alerting framework, capacity planning, and governance model for the Meet platform.
