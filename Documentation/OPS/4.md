# Meet v1.0

# Operations & Monitoring Manual (OPS)

## OPS - Part 4

## Capacity Planning, Performance Operations & Operational Governance

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Operations & Monitoring Manual

**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the operational strategy for capacity planning, performance management, SLA monitoring, operational governance, KPI reporting, compliance reviews, and continuous operational improvement for the Meet platform.

The objective is to ensure that the platform remains scalable, reliable, and operationally efficient as user demand grows.

---

# 2. Operational Objectives

Operations shall ensure:

* Sustainable Capacity
* Stable Performance
* High Availability
* Efficient Resource Utilization
* SLA Compliance
* Operational Transparency
* Continuous Improvement

Operational planning shall anticipate future growth.

---

# 3. Capacity Planning

Capacity planning shall evaluate:

* Active Users
* Concurrent Sessions
* Database Growth
* Storage Growth
* Queue Throughput
* Media Growth
* Voice Call Capacity
* Video Call Capacity

Capacity forecasts shall be reviewed regularly.

---

# 4. Resource Management

Operational resources include:

* Compute Resources
* Memory
* Storage
* Database Connections
* Queue Workers
* Network Capacity

Resource utilization shall be monitored continuously.

---

# 5. Performance Operations

Operational performance shall monitor:

* API Response Time
* Database Query Time
* Queue Processing Time
* Scheduler Performance
* File Upload Performance
* Voice Call Quality
* Video Call Quality

Performance degradation shall trigger investigation.

---

# 6. SLA Monitoring

Service Level Objectives may include:

* Service Availability
* API Availability
* Incident Response Time
* Recovery Time
* Queue Availability
* Database Availability

SLA compliance shall be measured and reported.

---

# 7. KPI Management

Operational KPIs may include:

* Uptime
* Incident Count
* MTTR
* Queue Throughput
* API Latency
* Error Rate
* Backup Success Rate

KPIs shall support operational decision-making.

---

# 8. Operational Governance

Governance activities shall include:

* Change Management
* Configuration Reviews
* Capacity Reviews
* Security Reviews
* Performance Reviews
* Documentation Reviews

Operational governance shall be performed at defined intervals.

---

# 9. Compliance Monitoring

Operational compliance shall verify:

* Backup Completion
* Monitoring Availability
* Audit Logging
* Security Controls
* Operational Documentation
* Deployment Records

Compliance results shall be documented.

---

# 10. Operational Reviews

Review meetings shall include:

* Incident Review
* Capacity Review
* Performance Review
* Security Review
* Infrastructure Review
* Risk Review

Action items shall be tracked until completion.

---

# 11. Continuous Improvement

Operational improvements shall include:

* Process Optimization
* Infrastructure Optimization
* Automation
* Monitoring Enhancements
* Documentation Updates

Improvements shall be prioritized according to operational impact.

---

# 12. Operational Metrics

Metrics may include:

* Capacity Utilization
* Growth Rate
* Storage Consumption
* Queue Performance
* Deployment Success Rate
* Incident Trends

Metrics shall support long-term planning.

---

# 13. Reporting

Operational reporting shall provide:

* Weekly Operations Summary
* Monthly Capacity Report
* Quarterly Performance Review
* Annual Operational Assessment

Reports shall be retained for historical analysis.

---

# 14. Future Enhancements

The governance framework supports:

* AI-Assisted Capacity Planning
* Predictive Performance Analysis
* Automated SLA Reporting
* Cloud Resource Optimization
* Intelligent Cost Management

Future enhancements shall integrate without redesigning operational governance.

---

# 15. OPS-4 Summary

This document defines the operational strategy for capacity planning, resource management, performance operations, SLA monitoring, governance, KPI management, compliance monitoring, reporting, and continuous improvement for the Meet platform.
