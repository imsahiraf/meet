## Final Operations & Monitoring Blueprint

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document serves as the master Operations & Monitoring blueprint for the Meet platform.

It consolidates the operational architecture, monitoring strategy, incident management framework, capacity planning model, governance standards, and long-term operational objectives established throughout the OPS documentation.

This document defines the minimum operational standards for every production deployment.

---

# 2. Operations Matrix

| Operational Area       | Primary Objective                 |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Service Monitoring     | Continuous operational visibility |
| Health Checks          | Early issue detection             |
| Logging                | Troubleshooting and auditing      |
| Alerting               | Rapid incident notification       |
| Incident Management    | Structured response and recovery  |
| Capacity Planning      | Sustainable growth                |
| Performance Operations | Stable service performance        |
| Backup Operations      | Data protection                   |
| Operational Governance | Controlled operational processes  |
| Continuous Improvement | Long-term operational excellence  |

All operational areas shall function together as a unified production operations framework.

# 3. Operational Governance

Operational governance shall require:

* Daily Health Reviews
* Weekly Operational Reviews
* Monthly Capacity Reviews
* Quarterly Security Reviews
* Annual Disaster Recovery Validation

Governance activities shall be documented and tracked.

# 5. Operational Acceptance Criteria

Operations are considered production-ready only when:

* Monitoring is active.
* Health checks are functioning.
* Alerting is operational.
* Backup verification succeeds.
* Recovery procedures are validated.
* Documentation is current.
* Operational approvals are complete.

Mandatory operational controls shall not be bypassed.
