# Meet Mail - user guide

Open **Mail** in the Meet menu.

## Get started
- **Your free Meet address**: Mail > Mail settings > "Get your free Meet address", choose a name, Create. Your address
  is *yourname*.user@example.com (for example rahul.k.user@example.com). You get 30 MB free (Meet Insights customers get the size
  of their package). Meet Insights users can also create it, read it and send from it in Insights > Mail; both show the
  same mailbox.
- **Bring your other mail**: Mail settings > "Add an e-mail account". Pick Gmail, Yahoo, Rediffmail, Zoho, Outlook or
  "Other", type the e-mail and password, press **Test and add**. Most providers need an *app password* when two-step login
  is on (Gmail: myaccount.google.com > Security > App passwords; Yahoo: Account security > Generate app password; Zoho:
  My Account > Security > App passwords; Rediffmail: turn on IMAP access in settings first).
- **Connect Google (free, recommended)**: Mail settings > Connect Google. Your Gmail appears in Meet, older Meet mail
  moves to a "Meet Mail" folder in your Google Drive (your mailbox never fills up), and your Google contacts become
  address suggestions. Everything syncs every hour; "Sync now" does it at once.

## Reading
- Folders: Inbox, Starred, Sent, Drafts, Archive, Spam, Trash. Accounts and labels are listed below the folders.
- Search looks in the subject, sender and text. Tick messages to mark read / unread, star, archive, mark spam, delete or
  label many at once. Empty Trash deletes for good.
- Pictures from the internet are hidden until you press "Show pictures" (senders cannot see that you opened the mail).
- Mail moved to Google Drive opens from Drive when you click it ("in Drive" in the list).

## Writing
- **Compose**: choose the From address (any of your addresses), To / Cc / Bcc (suggestions from your contacts), subject,
  text with bold, italic, lists and links, attachments up to 20 MB. **Save draft** keeps it in Drafts.
- **Reply / Reply all / Forward** from an open message; forwarding keeps the attachments.
- Your signature (Mail settings) is added to new messages.

## Settings
- **Away message**: an automatic reply, at most once every 4 days per sender, until the date you choose.
- **Filters**: when From / To / Subject contains a word, add a label, move to archive / spam / trash, star or mark read.
- **Labels**: coloured labels for your own sorting.
- **Default address**: the From address that new messages start with.
- **Remove an account**: removes it and its copies from Meet; nothing is deleted at Gmail / Yahoo / Zoho.

## imsahiraf Insights users
- Open Meet from Insights (Apps > imsahiraf Meet): your Meet account is created automatically the first time.
- Already had a Meet account? Meet > Settings > imsahiraf Insights (`/settings/insights`) > **Link my Insights login**. After
  that, opening Meet from Insights signs you in to your own account with all your chats, contacts and mail.
