# Meet v1.0

# Brand Guidelines (BRD)

## BRD - Part 1

## Brand Identity & Design Principles

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Brand Guidelines

**Priority:** High

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the official brand identity, design principles, visual standards, communication style, and branding rules for the Meet platform.

Its objective is to ensure that every user-facing experience maintains a consistent, recognizable, and professional brand identity across web, mobile, documentation, and marketing materials.

---

# 2. Brand Vision

Meet is designed to provide secure, fast, reliable, and intuitive communication.

The brand shall represent:

* Trust
* Simplicity
* Privacy
* Innovation
* Professionalism
* Reliability
* Human Connection

All branding decisions shall reinforce these values.

---

# 3. Brand Personality

The Meet brand shall be perceived as:

* Modern
* Friendly
* Professional
* Secure
* Minimal
* Accessible
* Global

Visual and written communication shall remain consistent with this personality.

---

# 4. Brand Mission

The mission of Meet is:

> To enable secure, high-quality, and effortless digital communication while protecting user privacy and delivering a reliable collaboration experience.

---

# 5. Core Brand Values

The platform shall consistently emphasize:

* Security by Design
* User Privacy
* Simplicity
* Reliability
* Performance
* Accessibility
* Continuous Innovation

These values shall influence product, design, and communication decisions.

---

# 6. Logo Guidelines

The official Meet logo shall:

* Remain clear and legible.
* Maintain consistent proportions.
* Preserve required clear space.
* Not be stretched or distorted.
* Not be recolored outside approved palettes.
* Be used in approved file formats.

Only approved logo assets shall be distributed.

---

# 7. Color System

The design system shall define:

* Primary Brand Color
* Secondary Brand Color
* Accent Colors
* Success Color
* Warning Color
* Error Color
* Neutral Palette

Color usage shall comply with accessibility requirements.

---

# 8. Typography

Typography shall define:

* Primary Typeface
* Secondary Typeface
* Heading Scale
* Body Text Scale
* Button Typography
* Caption Typography

Typography shall remain consistent across all interfaces.

---

# 9. Iconography

Icons shall:

* Maintain a consistent visual style.
* Be simple and recognizable.
* Scale cleanly across supported devices.
* Support accessibility requirements.

Only approved icon sets shall be used.

---

# 10. Imagery

Images shall communicate:

* Collaboration
* Communication
* Diversity
* Professionalism
* Simplicity

Visual assets shall avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# 11. Illustration Style

Illustrations shall be:

* Modern
* Minimal
* Friendly
* Consistent
* Scalable

Illustrations shall support the overall product identity.

---

# 12. Brand Voice

Written communication shall be:

* Clear
* Helpful
* Respectful
* Professional
* Concise

Technical language shall be appropriate for the intended audience.

---

# 13. Accessibility

Brand implementation shall support:

* Readable Typography
* Color Contrast
* Keyboard Navigation
* Screen Reader Compatibility
* Inclusive Design

Accessibility shall remain a core brand principle.

---

# 14. Future Enhancements

The brand system supports future additions including:

* Motion Design Standards
* Animation Guidelines
* Audio Branding
* Marketing Templates
* Product Illustration Library

Future additions shall remain consistent with the established identity.

---

# 15. BRD-1 Summary

This document establishes the brand identity, design principles, visual language, typography, color system, accessibility standards, and communication style for the Meet platform.
