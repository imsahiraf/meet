# Meet v1.0

# Brand Guidelines (BRD)

## BRD - Part 2

## UI Branding, Marketing Assets & Brand Governance

**Version:** 1.0

**Document Type:** Brand Guidelines

**Priority:** High

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the branding standards for the user interface, marketing materials, product assets, documentation, and governance processes for the Meet platform.

The objective is to ensure consistent application of the Meet brand across all customer touchpoints.

---

# 2. UI Branding Standards

Every user interface shall maintain:

* Consistent Color Palette
* Standard Typography
* Uniform Iconography
* Consistent Spacing
* Standard Button Styles
* Unified Form Design
* Consistent Navigation

The UI shall follow the approved Design System (UDS).

---

# 3. Component Branding

The following components shall follow standardized branding:

* Login Screens
* Registration Screens
* Chat Interface
* Voice Call Interface
* Video Call Interface
* Notifications
* Dialogs
* Error Pages
* Settings Pages
* Administrative Dashboard

Brand consistency shall be maintained throughout the application.

---

# 4. Marketing Materials

Marketing assets shall include:

* Website
* Product Brochures
* Presentations
* Social Media Graphics
* Email Campaigns
* Product Videos
* Advertisements

All materials shall use approved brand assets.

---

# 5. Documentation Branding

Project documentation shall maintain:

* Standard Cover Page
* Version Information
* Document Headers
* Typography Standards
* Consistent Formatting
* Revision History

Documentation shall reflect the official Meet identity.

---

# 6. Social Media Guidelines

Official social media communication shall be:

* Professional
* Informative
* Friendly
* Consistent
* Brand Aligned

Visual identity shall remain consistent across platforms.

---

# 7. Brand Asset Management

Approved assets shall include:

* Logos
* Icons
* Illustrations
* Color Palette
* Typography Files
* Templates

Only approved asset repositories shall be used.

---

# 8. Brand Governance

Brand governance shall require:

* Design Review
* Marketing Review
* Product Review
* Documentation Review
* Final Approval

Brand changes shall follow documented approval processes.

---

# 9. Brand Approval Workflow

```text id="brandapprovalworkflow"
Design Created
      ↓
Brand Review
      ↓
Revision (if required)
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Publication
```

Only approved materials shall be published.

---

# 10. Brand Compliance

Compliance reviews shall verify:

* Logo Usage
* Typography
* Color Accuracy
* Accessibility
* Messaging
* Asset Consistency

Non-compliant materials shall be updated before publication.

---

# 11. Communication Standards

External communication shall maintain:

* Clear Language
* Professional Tone
* Accurate Information
* Respectful Messaging
* Inclusive Language

Brand communication shall reinforce user trust.

---

# 12. Asset Versioning

Brand assets shall include:

* Version Identifier
* Release Date
* Usage Notes
* Approval Status

Obsolete assets shall be archived.

---

# 13. Future Brand Evolution

The branding framework supports:

* Design System Expansion
* Motion Branding
* Interactive Marketing Assets
* Brand Localization
* Product Family Branding

Future evolution shall preserve brand recognition.

---

# 14. Brand Review Process

Periodic reviews shall evaluate:

* Product Consistency
* Marketing Consistency
* Documentation Consistency
* Accessibility Compliance
* User Feedback

Review outcomes shall guide future brand improvements.

---

# 15. Brand Completion Summary

The Brand Guidelines consist of:

* BRD-1 - Brand Identity & Design Principles
* BRD-2 - UI Branding, Marketing Assets & Brand Governance

Together these documents define the complete visual identity, communication standards, branding governance, and asset management framework for the Meet platform.
