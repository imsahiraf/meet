# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

# Chapter 17 - Analytics, Reporting & Monitoring

---

# Module Overview

The Analytics, Reporting & Monitoring module provides comprehensive operational, business, and technical insights for the Meet platform.

The module enables administrators to monitor platform usage, user engagement, meetings, chats, calls, storage, security, background jobs, and overall system health through configurable dashboards and reports.

All analytics collection shall respect user privacy settings and applicable legal requirements.

# Chapter Summary

The Analytics, Reporting & Monitoring module provides complete visibility into the Meet platform through operational dashboards, business reports, performance metrics, security analytics, storage monitoring, queue and scheduler diagnostics, and configurable alerts. It establishes a scalable analytics foundation while maintaining privacy, administrative control, and future readiness for advanced business intelligence capabilities.
