# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

# Chapter 23 - Future Roadmap & AI Readiness

---

# Module Overview

This chapter defines the long-term vision for the Meet platform beyond Version 1.

These requirements are not mandatory for the initial release but establish architectural goals so that future enhancements can be implemented without major redesign.

Version 1 shall be developed with extensibility in mind, allowing additional modules and services to be integrated incrementally.

---

# FR-361 - AI Meeting Summary

## Purpose

Automatically generate summaries after completed meetings.

---

## Future Features

* Key Discussion Points
* Action Items
* Decisions Made
* Participant Summary
* Timeline Summary

---

## Business Rules

Version 1 shall expose extension points to support future AI integration without requiring structural changes to the Meeting Engine.

# Architectural Principles

Future enhancements shall:

* Preserve backward compatibility where practical.
* Reuse existing APIs and service layers.
* Respect privacy and authorization rules.
* Avoid major database redesign whenever possible.
* Follow the modular architecture defined throughout this specification.

---

# Chapter Summary

This chapter establishes the long-term product vision for Meet. It defines AI readiness, extensibility, enterprise capabilities, cloud integrations, native client support, plugin architecture, and future collaboration features. While these capabilities are outside the scope of Version 1, the current architecture shall remain prepared to incorporate them with minimal disruption, ensuring that the platform can evolve over time without sacrificing maintainability or scalability.
