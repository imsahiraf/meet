# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

# Chapter 18 - Deployment, Operations & Disaster Recovery

---

# Module Overview

The Deployment, Operations & Disaster Recovery module defines how the Meet platform shall be deployed, configured, maintained, monitored, backed up, upgraded, and recovered in production.

Version 1 shall support deployment on standard cPanel hosting while maintaining a migration path for future VPS, dedicated servers, Docker, and cloud platforms.

The deployment architecture shall prioritize reliability, maintainability, security, and minimal downtime.

---

# Chapter Summary

The Deployment, Operations & Disaster Recovery module defines the operational foundation of the Meet platform. It covers deployment on cPanel, environment configuration, installation, migrations, backups, restoration, scheduler and queue management, optimization, logging, monitoring, maintenance, disaster recovery, and operational documentation. The architecture is designed to support reliable production deployments today while remaining ready for future cloud and enterprise environments.
