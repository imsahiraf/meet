# FR-001 - User Registration

## Purpose

The system shall allow a guest user to register using:

* Phone Number
* Email Address
* Password

---

## Actor

**Guest**

---

## Priority

**Critical**

---

## Preconditions

* User must not already exist.

---

## Workflow

1. Guest opens the Registration page.
2. Enters:

   * Phone Number
   * Email Address
   * Password
   * Confirm Password
3. Accepts Terms & Conditions.
4. Clicks **Register**.
5. Laravel validates the request.
6. User account is created.
7. Email OTP is generated.
8. OTP is sent to the registered email address.
9. User is redirected to the **Verify Email** screen.

---

## Validation Rules

### Phone Number

* Required
* Unique
* 10-15 digits
* International format ready

### Email Address

* Required
* Unique
* RFC-compliant
* Stored in lowercase

### Password

* Minimum: 8 characters
* Maximum: 64 characters
* Must contain:

  * Uppercase letter
  * Lowercase letter
  * Number
  * Special character
* Password confirmation required

---

## Business Rules

* Email verification is mandatory.
* Phone verification is reserved for a future version.
* Account remains inactive until email verification is completed.
* Maximum OTP verification attempts: **5**
* Maximum OTP resend attempts: **5**
* OTP expires after **10 minutes** (configurable).

# FR-002 - Email Verification

## Purpose

Verify ownership of the registered email address.

---

## Actor

**Registered User**

---

## Workflow

1. User enters the received OTP.
2. Laravel validates the OTP.
3. If valid:

   * Account is activated.
   * User is automatically logged in.
   * User is redirected to the dashboard.

---

## Validation Rules

* 6-digit numeric OTP
* OTP expiration validation
* Maximum verification attempts enforced

---

## Business Rules

* Expired OTP cannot be used.
* Generating a new OTP invalidates all previous OTPs.
* After five failed verification attempts, the user is temporarily blocked.

---

## Events

* EmailVerified

---

## Event Listeners

* WelcomeEmail
* ActivityLog

---

## Database Tables

* email_otps
* users
* activity_logs

---

## Acceptance Criteria

* Email successfully verified.
* Account activated.
* User automatically logged in.

# FR-003 - Resend OTP

## Purpose

Generate and send a new OTP.

---

## Business Rules

* Cooldown: **60 seconds**
* Maximum resend attempts: **5 per day**
* All limits must be configurable.

---

## Security Requirements

* Rate Limiting
* Future CAPTCHA support

---

## Acceptance Criteria

* New OTP sent successfully.
* Previous OTP becomes invalid.
* Activity is logged.
